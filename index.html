Why Nabta

Short lists, on purpose.

Nabta started in a rented studio in Jabal Al Weibdeh, out of plain frustration. We were tired of buying products with thirty ingredients and no way to tell which two were actually doing anything.

So our formulas are short. Cold-pressed carrier oils, a handful of botanical extracts, and actives at a level that earns its place. We blend a few hundred bottles at a time, fill them by hand, and write the date on the base. No synthetic fragrance, and no claims we'd be embarrassed to defend.

The Nabta routine

Three steps. Two minutes.

01 · Prep

Wake the skin up

A few sweeps of Damask Rose Essence over clean skin. It rehydrates and gives everything after it something to sink into.

02 · Treat

Feed it what it needs

Two or three drops of Calendula Barrier Serum, pressed in. This is the step that does the quiet, long-term work.

03 · Seal

Lock it all in

Warm three drops of Rosehip Renewal Oil between your palms and press over the top. Skin still feels soft by morning, even through an Amman winter.

Sourcing

Grown close to home.

Where we can, we buy from the region. Damask rose distilled near the Jordan Valley, olive oil pressed an hour north, prickly pear from two growers we know by name. It travels less, and you can smell how fresh it is the moment you open the bottle.

What we can't get locally, we buy from suppliers who send harvest dates and certificates without us having to chase them. Traceability isn't a tagline here. It's just how we keep ourselves honest.
