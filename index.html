Why Nabta

Short lists, on purpose.

Nabta started in a rented studio in Jabal Al Weibdeh. Our founder had spent six years formulating for large brands and kept seeing products with thirty ingredients where two did the work.

So our formulas are short. Cold-pressed carrier oils, a handful of botanical extracts, and actives at a level that earns its place. We blend around 300 bottles at a time, fill them by hand, and print the batch date on the base. No synthetic fragrance, no essential oils above 0.5%.

The Nabta routine

Three steps. Two minutes.

01 · Prep

Wake the skin up

A few sweeps of Damask Rose Essence over clean skin. It rehydrates and gives everything after it something to sink into.

02 · Treat

Feed it what it needs

Two or three drops of Calendula Barrier Serum, pressed in. This is the step that does the quiet, long-term work.

03 · Seal

Lock it all in

Warm three drops of Rosehip Renewal Oil between your palms and press over the top. Skin still feels soft by morning, even through an Amman winter.

Sourcing

Grown close to home.

Where we can, we buy from the region. Damask rose water from a distiller in Deir Alla, olive oil from a press outside Ajloun, prickly pear seed oil from two growers near Madaba.

Rosehip, sea buckthorn and neroli come from certified suppliers in Chile, Finland and Tunisia. Every drum arrives with a harvest date and a certificate of analysis, and we keep both on file for each batch.
