Why Flora

Fewer ingredients, chosen well.

We started Flora in a small studio in Jabal Al Weibdeh, frustrated by shelves of products that promised everything and respected nothing. Our formulas are short by design: cold-pressed carrier oils, botanical extracts, and actives at levels that actually do the work.

Everything is blended in batches of a few hundred, hand-filled, and dated. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no claims we can't stand behind.

The Flora ritual

Three steps, morning and night.

01 — Cleanse

Melt the day away

Warm the Calendula balm between your palms and massage over dry skin. It lifts sunscreen and grime without stripping.

02 — Tone

Restore the balance

Two presses of Neroli mist to rehydrate and prep the skin for whatever comes next. A quiet moment in the routine.

03 — Nourish

Seal it in

Three drops of Rosehip oil, pressed in. Skin looks rested by morning and stays soft through the dry Amman winter.

Sourcing

Grown close to home.

Where we can, we source from the region — Damask rose distilled near the Jordan Valley, olive oil pressed an hour north, prickly pear from small growers we know by name. It travels less, and it shows in the freshness.

What we can't grow here, we buy from suppliers who share their harvest dates and certificates. Traceability isn't a marketing line for us; it's how we keep quality honest.
