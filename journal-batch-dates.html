Why we date every bottle

How small-batch production works in the Weibdeh studio, and why each bottle has a date on the base.

By Dana Qasem · 14 July 2025

Every Nabta bottle has a batch date printed on the base. It tells you when the product was made, and tells us which drums of oil went into it.

How a batch works

We make each product every six to eight weeks, in batches of around 300 bottles. A batch takes a day to blend and a second day to fill, cap and label by hand.

Why it matters

Plant oils and extracts are at their best in the first months after they are made. Small batches mean most bottles reach customers within three weeks of filling.

What to look for

Use a product within 12 months of the batch date, and within 6 months of opening it. If a date is more than a year old, message us and we will replace the bottle.
