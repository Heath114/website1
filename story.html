A short list, chosen well

Nabta began in 2019 as three blends made on a kitchen table in Weibdeh and sold at a Friday market.

The beginning

From a kitchen table in Weibdeh

Our founder, Dana Qasem, trained as a pharmacist and then spent six years formulating for bigger beauty brands. She left in 2018, wanting to make products with fewer ingredients and higher concentrations of the ones that work.

Nabta started as a few blends she mixed for friends with reactive skin. By the end of the first year those friends had sent over two hundred people to the market stall, and Dana signed her first stockist.

There are six of us now, and everything is still blended, filled and labelled in the Weibdeh studio.

What we hold to

Four things we won't move on

01

Short formulas

If an ingredient isn't pulling its weight, it doesn't go in. Most of our bottles list under twelve things, and we can tell you what each one does.

02

Small batches

We make a few hundred at a time and date every bottle. Fresher actives, and nothing sitting in a warehouse for two years going quietly stale.

03

Traceable sourcing

Rose water from Deir Alla, olive oil from Ajloun, prickly pear from Madaba. For everything else we keep the harvest date and supplier certificate on file for each batch.

04

No added fragrance

What you smell comes from the botanicals themselves. Nothing in the range contains synthetic perfume.

The road so far

Seven years in Weibdeh

2019

First three blends sold at the Friday market in Weibdeh. Forty bottles, sold out by noon.

2021

Signed the lease on the studio and its little storefront. Started distilling Damask rose with a grower near the Jordan Valley.

2023

Reached fifteen stockists across Amman, Madaba and Aqaba. Launched refills at the studio: bring back a bottle and get 3 JD off.

Today

Six people, eight products and about 1,200 bottles a month.
