A short list, chosen well

Flora began the way most good things do — out of a small frustration, and a kitchen table covered in oils.

The beginning

From a kitchen table in Weibdeh

Our founder, Dana Qasem, trained as a pharmacist before spending six years formulating for larger beauty brands. She left because the briefs kept getting longer and the ingredient lists kept getting worse — more claims, more filler, less of what actually helped skin.

Flora started as a handful of blends made for friends with sensitive, reactive skin. When people kept coming back — and bringing others — it became something worth doing properly.

Today we're a team of six, still small by design, still making everything ourselves in a studio you could walk across in ten steps.

What we hold to

Four things we won't compromise

01

Short formulas

If an ingredient isn't doing real work, it doesn't go in. Most of our products list under twelve components.

02

Small batches

We make in batches of a few hundred and date every bottle. Fresher actives, and nothing sitting in a warehouse for years.

03

Honest sourcing

Regional where we can, traceable always. We'll tell you where a botanical came from and when it was harvested.

04

No fragrance theatre

Scent comes from the botanicals themselves — never synthetic perfume added to make a product "feel" like it works.

The road so far

A few years, quietly

2019

First three products sold at a Friday market in Weibdeh. Sold out by noon.

2021

Opened our studio and small storefront. Began distilling Damask rose with a grower near the Jordan Valley.

2023

Reached fifteen stockists across Amman, Madaba and Aqaba. Launched refills to cut packaging waste.

Today

A team of six, a twelve-product range, and the same short list we started with.
