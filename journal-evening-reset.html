The two-minute evening routine

For late nights. The two steps worth doing when you have no time for the rest.

By Reem Khalil · 9 February 2026

On some nights a full routine is not going to happen. These are the two steps to keep, in order of importance.

1. Take everything off

Sunscreen, makeup and the day's pollution should not stay on overnight. If you only do one thing, massage a few pumps of cleansing oil over dry skin for thirty seconds, then rinse with warm water.

2. Put one thing back

While your face is still damp, press three drops of Rosehip Renewal Oil into your skin. It holds in the water from rinsing and does the work of a moisturiser.

What you can skip

Toner, essence, eye oil and any active serums can wait until tomorrow. Skipping them for a night makes no measurable difference.
